About Aarya

A thoughtful creative partner, working from India for USA small businesses.

Founder

Atul Ganpatrao Kalukhe

Aarya Web Developers was founded by Atul Ganpatrao Kalukhe to make professional creative support and modern websites more accessible to small businesses across the United States.

Based in India, Aarya is built around clear communication, practical collaboration, and work that serves a real business purpose—not generic decoration. From an individual poster to a complete website, every project begins with understanding what the customer needs to communicate and what action the audience should take.
